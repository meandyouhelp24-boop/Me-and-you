📋 Moderator guide

Your job: keep Me & You kind and safe, especially for under-18s. You never see who wrote a post, and you must never try to find out or tell anyone.

How fast: check the queue at least once a day. Reports about safety come first.

Approve when it is kind, honest, and has no private details. Reject when it has unkind or hateful words, bullying, contact details (phone, email, social media, links), real names, addresses or schools, sexual content, advertising, or anything that could harm someone.

Someone sounds in danger (talking about suicide, self-harm, abuse): do not just reject and move on. Tell the person who runs the app straight away. Do not approve posts that give methods. Members can find helplines under Safety & Support.

A child may be at risk, or an adult is trying to contact a young member: reject the post, pause the author for 30 days or longer, and tell the person who runs the app the same day. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services.

Pausing a member: use "Pause this member" on a post, comment or report. Suggested steps: first minor breach, 1 day. Repeat, 7 days. Serious or deliberate, 30 days. Very serious (grooming, threats, sharing private details of others), until the person who runs the app reviews it. Always write a short reason. The member is told only that something they shared broke the rules.

Fairness: treat everyone the same. If you are not sure, reject and ask the person who runs the app. Lift a pause from the Paused members tab if it was a mistake.

Community rules & privacy 📜

Me & You works when people feel safe. Please read this before you share.

Our community rules

1. Be kind. Bullying, name-calling, mocking and hate are never allowed.

2. Protect privacy. Never share your own or anyone else's full name, address, school, phone number, social media or other details that could identify someone.

3. Respect every story. People have different lives and experiences. Listen without judging.

4. Keep it safe. No sexual content, threats, or anything that encourages harm.

5. Be honest. Do not pretend to be someone else.

6. Respect decisions. Moderators may reject or remove content that breaks these rules.

How we keep Me & You kind

New posts and comments are checked by a moderator before other people can see them. Unkind words are blocked automatically. You can report anything with ⚑ Report, and block members with 🚫 Block. Members who break the rules can be paused (they can read but not post) or removed.

Your information

What we keep: your email (to log in), your nickname, age range, and optional country, plus what you create: posts, comments, reactions, saves, collections, followed topics and diary entries.

What others can see: your nickname on questions, skills and messages. Stories are shown as anonymous, with only an age range and country, so people can see they are not alone. Members under 18 appear as "a young member" on questions, skills and messages. Contact details such as phone numbers, emails and social media names are blocked. Your email is never shown to other members.

Speaking instead of typing: if you use the microphone button, your phone's browser may send your voice to its speech service to turn it into words. Me & You does not record or keep your voice, only the words that appear.

Your diary: it is not visible to other members or moderators in the app. It is stored online, and the person who runs the app can technically access stored data, so please avoid very sensitive details.

Deleting your account: go to Account and tap "Delete my account". Your profile, posts, comments, diary, collections and drafts are permanently deleted straight away.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are: Me & You is a student project that gives people a safe, anonymous space to share experiences. Contact: meandyou.help24@gmail.com

Who can join: Me & You is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

What we collect: your email, password (stored securely, we cannot read it), nickname, age range and optional country, plus what you create: stories, questions, skills, messages, comments, reactions, saves, collections, followed topics, drafts and diary entries.

Why we use it: to run your account, show your posts to the community, keep members safe through moderation, and send you updates about your posts. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who can see it: Stories are shown as anonymous, with only an age range and country. Questions, skills and messages show your nickname (members under 18 appear as "a young member"). Moderators review posts before they appear and cannot see who wrote a story. Your email is never shown to other members. Your diary is private in the app.

Services we use: Supabase stores our data online. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a real person. Photos and fonts load from Unsplash and Google Fonts, which may see your device's IP address. If you use the microphone button, your browser may send your voice to its own speech service. We do not record or keep your voice.

On your device: the app saves some things in your browser, such as drafts and text size, so it works as you expect.

How long we keep it: until you delete it or delete your account.

Your rights: you can see, correct and delete your information. Edit your profile and posts in the app, or go to Account and tap "Delete my account" to permanently delete everything linked to you. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for help.

Safety: Me & You is not a crisis service. If you are in danger, use the Safety & Support page or call your emergency services.

Complaints: if you are in Ireland, you can contact the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Changes: if this policy changes, we will update the date above.
